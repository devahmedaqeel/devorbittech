Who We Are & Our Founding Vision

Dev Orbit Tech was founded by Engr. Ahmed Aqeel, a software engineer with a passion for building robust digital products and empowering technical talent.

Engr. Ahmed Aqeel

CEO & Founder

Headquartered at the Software Technology Park, University of Kotli (AJK), Dev Orbit Tech has grown into a collaborative engineering house serving commercial clients globally while mentoring future software engineers.

Our core mission is straightforward: Deliver clean, maintainable, and high-performance digital products that scale smoothly with business growth. Whether developing AI-powered healthcare tools like MediReport AI or high-traffic e-commerce systems like Blissful Blinds, we adhere to strict engineering standards, transparent communication, and pragmatic delivery timelines.
