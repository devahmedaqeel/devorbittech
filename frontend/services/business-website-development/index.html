Business Website Development Services

We engineer custom, conversion-focused business websites that turn casual search visitors into paying customers and qualified project inquiries. Clean code, sub-second load times, and structured technical SEO designed for long-term commercial growth.

Direct Answer / Service Overview:

Dev Orbit Tech builds bespoke business websites engineered specifically around your commercial goals. Unlike generic slow page-builder templates, our business websites feature custom semantic architecture, responsive layouts, interactive quote funnels, seamless WhatsApp integrations, and structured data that helps your business stand out on Google, Bing, and AI search engines.

Who This Service Is For

  • Service Businesses: Contractors, consultancies, legal, healthcare, and agencies needing trustworthy digital presentation.
  • Growing Small & Medium Enterprises: Companies looking to outgrow basic social media pages with an authoritative owned web property.
  • Startups & Founders: New ventures that need an immediate commercial presence to attract early clients and partners.

Business Problems We Solve

  • High Mobile Bounce Rates: Bloated templates that take 5+ seconds to load drive customers straight to competitors. We guarantee sub-second performance.
  • Low Inquiry Conversions: Confusing navigation and buried forms. We design friction-free inquiry funnels and one-tap WhatsApp chat hooks.
  • Invisibility in Search: Missing meta tags and schema markup. We implement structured Schema.org and geo-targeted metadata.
  • Lack of Professional Credibility: Broken mobile layouts erode trust. We deliver polished, dark or light mode responsive aesthetics.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a modern business website include?

A modern business website must have a clear value proposition above the fold, dedicated service detail pages, proof of past work (case studies and portfolio), fast mobile loading speeds, accessible contact forms, direct messaging links (WhatsApp), and structured Schema.org metadata.

How much does a business website cost?

Costs vary based on scope, feature complexity, and custom design needs. A high-performance custom business site ranges from lean single-page landers to comprehensive multi-page platforms. We provide transparent, milestone-based quotes after scoping your requirements.

How long does it take to develop?

Most business websites are planned, engineered, tested, and deployed within 2 to 4 weeks. We work in rapid agile sprints with continuous staging access so you can review progress in real time.

Can you redesign an existing slow website?

Yes. We rebuild outdated, slow WordPress or Wix sites into high-velocity modern web platforms, preserving your search rankings and migrating your content with zero downtime.
