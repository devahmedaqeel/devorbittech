Ahmed Aqeel is the driving force behind Dev Orbit Tech. As a modern technology startup founder, Engr. Ahmed Aqeel is focused on delivering innovative digital solutions and empowering students across Pakistan with future-ready technical skills.

Our company works in the fields of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Web Development, Mobile Application Development, Graphic Designing, Software Documentation, and Digital Innovation.

We aim to create a professional technology environment where students can learn practical skills, explore modern technologies, and prepare themselves for the digital future.
